Triennial 2025: The Exchange

One visual identity across a citywide public art exhibition. Working from Siena Scarff Design's brand guidelines, I adapted the More ___ system for bus wraps, digital kiosks, A-frames, window posters, and a map keying twenty artist locations across nine neighborhoods. Formats reused or rebuilt the system according to viewing distance and information load. After the Triennial closed, I updated the window poster shown here to keep directing visitors to Swoon's installation.

Design and production: Keegan Andrulis. Brand guidelines by Siena Scarff Design. Produced in-house at the Boston Public Art Triennial, 2025.

Making Public

Print and digital collateral for Making Public, the Triennial’s October 2025 convening at Roxbury Community College. Source imagery came from a youth workshop led by Ñ Press with NUBE East Boston for Gabriel Sosa’s Triennial project. I adapted it across posters, wayfinding, a program handout, QR flyer, newsletter ad, and event banner.

Design: Keegan Andrulis. Source imagery created at a youth workshop with Ñ Press and NUBE East Boston, part of Gabriel Sosa’s project Ñ Press. Event photography: Annielly Camargo. Produced in-house at the Boston Public Art Triennial, 2025.
