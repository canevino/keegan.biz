Triennial

2025

campaign · print · environmental

A citywide campaign built from the Boston Public Art Triennial’s established identity. I adapted the “More ____” system across bus wraps, digital kiosks, A-frames, window posters, maps and other public-facing formats.

Some pieces reused existing layouts while others were new compositions within the system. The goal was consistency without flattening every format into the same solution.

Beyond the core campaign, I also adapted the brand for individual events and exhibitions. That included Art + Stroll, Public Processes with Boston Cyberarts, and other pieces that needed to feel distinct while remaining recognizably part of the Triennial.

Design and production: Keegan Andrulis. Brand guidelines by Siena Scarff Design. Produced in-house at the Boston Public Art Triennial, 2025.

identity · collateral · signage

Better Angels was a participatory pop-up installation by Boston artist Heather Kapplow. I worked with the artist to build the visual identity and with fabricators to produce the physical takeaways and signage.

The identity ran across event flyers, rack cards, interpretive and directional signage, a T-shirt and a preview render. Several pieces carried a custom map illustration and a clip-art-style drawing of the Better Angels inflatables.

The takeaways became the center of the collateral system. The Heaven Membership Card worked as a paper whistle that visitors could use to “call the angels,” while a separate foldout carried the project information and instructions and stood upright like a set of gates.

Identity, collateral and map illustration: Keegan Andrulis. Installation by Heather Kapplow. Flyer illustration by Heather Kapplow. Documentation photography by Mel Taing. Commissioned by the Boston Public Art Triennial.

Design: Keegan Andrulis. Source image created at a youth workshop with Ñ Press and NUBE East Boston.

Other work

2019–2025

poster series · concert promotion

Three posters for the Paul Winter Consort. Each began with finding an image that had a real relationship to the music, rather than treating photography as generic decoration.

Adventures in the Universe

A Yale concert built around Charles Ives and his unfinished Universe Symphony. NASA’s Hubble Ultra Deep Field supplied the image, with condensed type set against an enormous field of space.

Spring Equinox Celebration

A NOAA satellite image of the earth at the vernal equinox, paired with type curving around the globe and a visual reference to the Consort’s late-60s and 70s album language.

This Glorious Earth

An Earth Day concert at the Cathedral of St. John the Divine, using archival photography from the Consort’s Crestone, Colorado archive.

Design: Keegan Andrulis, for the Paul Winter Consort, 2023–2024.

Design work for Trinity’s student-run radio station, including a new logo, broadcast graphics and social media assets. The identity combines a sound wave with a broadcast signal and was carried through seasonal variants.

Fullam campaign

A social-first campaign for Gabi Fullam’s Trinity College Dublin Students’ Union presidential run. The visual system combined portrait photography, manifesto graphics and motion-first social assets.

A wine-label series developed for a marketing and branding elective. Each varietal received a distinct illustration, palette and cap treatment while sharing one label system.
