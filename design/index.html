01

More ____

Boston Public Art Triennial

Campaign design for the Triennial’s citywide exhibition, adapting the existing identity across print, advertising, wayfinding, and environmental applications.

Campaign system

Working from the Triennial’s established identity, I adapted the More ____ campaign across formats that had to work at very different scales: street posters, event graphics, A-frames, buses, digital kiosks, and exhibition collateral.

Some pieces were direct adaptations of existing campaign layouts; others were new compositions built inside the same system. The typography, color, and image treatment stay consistent while the hierarchy changes with the viewing distance and amount of information.

02

Better Angels

Boston Public Art Triennial

Identity, collateral, and signage for Heather Kapplow’s participatory installation, developed in collaboration with the artist.

identity + participation

Better Angels was a participatory installation by Heather Kapplow. I worked with Heather on the identity and designed the printed system around the encounter: the flyer, foldout takeaway, script and membership card, interpretive signage, wayfinding, and staff T-shirt.

The pieces had to do more than announce the project. They guided visitors through it, gave staff a working script, gathered responses, and sent people away with something they had physically used.

The staff script and Heaven Membership Card turn the identity into part of the performance. Visitors complete the card, then use it as a paper whistle to call the angels.

05

In use

The graphic system sits inside the performance rather than around it: on staff, clipboards, handouts, signs, and the objects visitors leave with.

Identity, collateral, map illustration, and production: Keegan Andrulis, in collaboration with Heather Kapplow. Installation by Heather Kapplow. Commissioned by the Boston Public Art Triennial.
