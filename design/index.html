01

More ____

Boston Public Art Triennial

A citywide campaign system adapted across print, environmental graphics, wayfinding, and digital formats.

A citywide campaign built from the Boston Public Art Triennial’s established identity. I adapted the “More ____” system across bus wraps, digital kiosks, A-frames, window posters, maps and other public-facing formats.

Some pieces reused existing layouts while others were new compositions within the system. The goal was consistency without flattening every format into the same solution.

Beyond the core campaign, I also adapted the brand for individual events and exhibitions including Art + Stroll and Public Processes.

Design and production: Keegan Andrulis. Brand guidelines by Siena Scarff Design. Produced in-house at the Boston Public Art Triennial.

02

Better Angels

Boston Public Art Triennial

Identity, collateral, and signage for a participatory installation, in collaboration with Heather Kapplow.

Better Angels was a participatory pop-up installation by Boston artist Heather Kapplow. I worked with Heather to build the visual identity and with fabricators to produce the physical takeaways and signage.

The identity ran across event flyers, rack cards, interpretive and directional signage, a T-shirt and a preview render.

The takeaways became the center of the collateral system. The Heaven Membership Card worked as a paper whistle that visitors could use to “call the angels,” while a separate foldout carried the project information and instructions.

Identity, collateral and map illustration: Keegan Andrulis. Installation by Heather Kapplow. Commissioned by the Boston Public Art Triennial.

Making Public

Boston Public Art Triennial

Event identity and collateral for a citywide convening, extending one source image across posters, wayfinding, programs, and digital formats.

Print and digital collateral for Making Public, the Triennial 2025 convening at Roxbury Community College. The image came from a youth workshop that Ñ Press ran with NUBE East Boston.

From there it expanded across posters, on-site wayfinding, a program handout, a QR flyer and newsletter and social formats.

Design: Keegan Andrulis. Source image created at a youth workshop with Ñ Press and NUBE East Boston.
