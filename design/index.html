Campaign identity

One visual identity across a citywide public art exhibition. Working from Siena Scarff Design's brand guidelines, I adapted the More ___ system for bus wraps, digital kiosks, A-frames, window posters, and a map keying twenty artist locations across nine neighborhoods. Formats reused or rebuilt the system according to viewing distance and information load. After the Triennial closed, I updated the window poster shown here to keep directing visitors to Swoon's installation.

Design and production: Keegan Andrulis. Brand guidelines by Siena Scarff Design. Produced in-house at the Boston Public Art Triennial, 2025.

Visual identity

Better Angels is a participatory pop-up installation by Boston artist Heather Kapplow, commissioned by the Boston Public Art Triennial: inflatable clouds, a set of heavenly gates, and a cast of angels who invite visitors to pause, connect, and reflect. I worked with the artist to build the visual identity, and with fabricators to produce the physical takeaways and signage.

The identity runs across event flyers, rack cards, interpretive and directional signage, a t-shirt, and a preview render. Some of it carries a custom map illustration I drew to locate the installation, along with a clip-art-style illustration of the Better Angels inflatables. The t-shirts I designed were hand tie-dyed by Triennial staff from my designs. The preview render started as a 3D model from the fabrication company Pneuhaus, which I edited into a preview image so the installation could be promoted before it was built.

The takeaways are the centerpiece of the collateral. The Heaven Membership Card is a working paper whistle that visitors use to “call the angels,” with a fill-in-the-blank interaction printed on it. It pairs with a separate foldout card that holds the whistle, folds up to stand on its own like a set of gates, and carries the project's information and the instructions for using the whistle. Both had to cut, fold, and function as objects, not just print flat.
