04 media identity · campaign graphics

PRX / Radiotopia

Ear Hustle, In the Queue, and Radiotopia

Editorial art, identity, and campaign graphics produced at PRX.

01

Ear Hustle

Ear Hustle is a Radiotopia show made inside San Quentin and a Pulitzer finalist. I wrote the pitch to Apple Podcasts for a feature on the show’s 2024 election episode, where the inside team runs a presidential poll at San Quentin. Apple selected it, and I built the hero art on a short turnaround from Damien Linnane’s existing pencil illustration, paired with a stars-and-stripes wordmark for the election theme.

02

Newsletter + social campaign

These two pieces sit best as a pair: one identity mark, one multi-show social graphic. The layout gives the logo room to breathe while treating the subscription graphic as the denser campaign object.

05 student media · campaigns · packaging

Trinity College Dublin

Trinity FM, Fullam Campaign, and Cane Vino

Identity, campaign, and packaging work from Trinity College Dublin.

01

Trinity FM

A wave-based identity for Trinity’s student radio station, carried across event graphics and social.

02

Fullam Campaign

A social-first campaign for Gabi Fullam’s run for Trinity College Dublin Students’ Union president, a race she won on the first count. The design centered on racial justice, accessibility, and supporting the most vulnerable students, using a steady green, purple, and white palette across portrait posters, manifesto cards, and motion.

03

Cane Vino

A wine label series made for a marketing and branding elective, built entirely from my own illustrations. Each varietal gets its own label while the overall system holds together through typography, bottle treatment, and controlled spacing.
