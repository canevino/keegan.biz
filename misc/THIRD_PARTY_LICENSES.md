# Open-source attribution

The interlace tracing model in `app.js` is an adaptation of the crossing, break, trace and redraw-overpass method in [Celtic Knot Maker](https://github.com/evoluteur/celtic-knot-maker) by Olivier Giulieri. The implementation was redesigned for random mixed-media ornaments, transformed and distorted shapes, and the host site's export and styling architecture. The underlying weave construction follows George Bain's *Celtic Art: The Methods of Construction*.

### Botanical growth methods

The added branching routines are original implementations of general L-system and space-colonization algorithms, developed after reviewing the public projects https://github.com/aurbano/TreeGenerator (MIT), https://github.com/TanviKumar/treegen, and https://github.com/Angramme/TreeGenerator. No source code or assets from these tree projects are redistributed.
