How the shapes work

Growth pattern controls the number, origins and direction of main stems (upright = one, paired = two, radiating = several sharing a base, etc.). Underlying geometry sets their construction curves. At 100% follow geometry, stems trace those curves exactly; at 0% they grow freely. Branches and foliage follow the plant species.

Ornamental transformation alters leaf contours, veins and attached flower details. Print wear controls local ink spread, missing pigment and slight plate misregistration.
