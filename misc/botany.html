how it works

Growth sets how many main stems grow and which routes they take. Shape supplies the underlying paths. Follow shape controls how closely the stems follow those paths. At 0 the stems grow freely; at 100 they follow the geometry precisely.

Ornament exaggerates leaf shapes and adds decorative color. Foliage changes leaf density. Ink wear now visibly changes abrasion, plate pressure, offset, and edge breakup in the printed marks.
