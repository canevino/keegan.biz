how it works ↗

Growth chooses the arrangement of stems. Construction determines the paths they follow. Follow geometry determines how closely they follow those paths. At 0 the stems grow freely; at 100 they follow the geometry precisely.

Ornament exaggerates leaf shapes and adds decorative color. Foliage changes leaf density. Ink wear changes the pressure and bleeding of the printed marks—not how much of the plant survives.
